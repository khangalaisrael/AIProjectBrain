# AI Project Brain brand

Part of the same family as VisionLearn and TalentScan, but with its own colour so each product is recognisable at a glance.

- **Idea:** a codebase is a graph. The logo is three linked nodes (one highlighted) on a teal tile, echoing the Atlas map.
- **Tagline:** Turn any codebase into a course you can actually follow.
- **Colours:** Teal `#0F766E` (light) / `#2DD4BF` (dark) is the primary. Coral `#FB7185` is the single highlight (the headline swipe and the top node), used once per screen. Ink on near-black in dark mode, white in light mode.
- **Voice:** plain and practical, like a senior engineer explaining a codebase. Short sentences, real examples, no hype.
- **Logo:** `components/brand/logo.tsx` (also `app/icon.svg` as the favicon). Minimum 16px; don't recolour the nodes.
